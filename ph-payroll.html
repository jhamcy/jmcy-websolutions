Everything included

  • Employee Management: Maintain employee profiles, employment details, and payroll-related settings.
  • Attendance & Time Records: Work with attendance entries and supported spreadsheet imports; calculate relevant time values such as overtime, tardiness, and undertime.
  • Payroll Processing: Process selected cutoff periods with applicable pay, allowances, overtime, and deductions.
  • Government Contribution Support: Configurable calculations for SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG/HDMF, and withholding-tax estimates.
  • Contribution Links Dashboard: A dedicated dashboard area with quick links to relevant government contribution websites, making it easier to open the portals used for contribution-related tasks.
  • Employee Loans: Maintain loan information and associated payroll deductions where configured.
  • Holiday & Wage Settings: Manage settings used by payroll calculations.
  • Payslips & Reports: Generate payroll-related documents for employee communication and recordkeeping.
  • Payroll History: Review previous payroll runs and related records.
  • License Activation: JMCY PH Payroll System license activation support.

Feature availability and exact behavior should be confirmed against the installed version and your business workflow before purchase.

A clear process for each payroll period

Use a consistent workflow to prepare employee data, review time records, and produce payroll outputs.

Step 1

Set up company and employees

Enter company details, maintain employee information, and review wage and contribution settings.

Step 2

Review attendance and cutoff

Check attendance records and select the payroll period before running calculations.

Step 3

Calculate and verify payroll

Review calculated amounts and deductions, then generate payslips and payroll reports.

Important: Payroll and government contribution rules can change. Review settings and verify all calculated amounts before paying employees or submitting official reports. Reports assist with preparation and recordkeeping; they do not replace employer review or official filing responsibilities.
